✦ Birthstones vs. Zodiac Stones ✦

Both offer a personal way to connect with a gemstone — but they're assigned differently. Knowing the difference helps you (and your customers) pick the version that means the most.

💎 Birthstones

Assignment: Based on calendar month

Origins: Historical & religious, often linked to the Breastplate of Aaron

Common use: Personalized jewelry & gifts — sweet 16 rings, mother's necklaces

♈ Zodiac Stones

Assignment: Based on astrological sign

Origins: Astrological & cultural, tied to celestial beliefs

Common use: Astrological & spiritual purposes — energy alignment, sign traits

The overlap: the two often connect — Garnet is both January's birthstone and an alternate zodiac stone for Aquarius, for example. When they line up, your gemstone choice resonates with both your birth month and your sign.

🌙

Birthstone Benefits

Strengthens identity and connection to your birth month. Each stone carries its own meaning — protection, love, courage — for daily inspiration.

✨

Zodiac Stone Benefits

Aligns with your sign to enhance positive traits and balance energy. Supports personal growth, self-awareness, and a deeper spiritual connection.
